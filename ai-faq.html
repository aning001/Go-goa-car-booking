  1. Navigate to Go Goa's interactive online Booking Form or rate estimator.
  2. Pick your desired car model, rental start/end dates, and preferred delivery hotspots.
  3. Submit your telephone details. Our client representative will reach out on WhatsApp/Call to confirm document verification.

Benefit: Direct booking is 100% commission-free with absolutely zero hidden state charges or middleman fees.

Our physical rental terminal desk is officially located at:
Counter 15, Manohar International Airport (MOPA)
Kasarvarne, Pernem District, Goa 403512, India.
We deliver vehicle handovers directly within the arrival flight parking zone block 24/7. When your flight touches down, walk straight to Counter 15; your assigned driver will hand over physical ignition keys immediately.

To complete safety verification and state compliance checks, the following two documents are strictly mandatory:

  • A Original Indian Driving License: Laminated, authorized category, non-learner type.
  • A Valid Government-Issued Aadhaar Card: Serves as biometric and residence verification.

Our agent will run a rapid 5-minute digital check on these documents at the precise moment of car handover. Local Goa regulations restrict bookings if original documents are unavailable.
