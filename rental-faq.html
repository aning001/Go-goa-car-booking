Core Rental & Travel Q&As

Q1: How do I book a premium self-drive car rental in Goa with Go Goa?

To secure a premium self drive car rental in Goa, complete these rapid steps:

  • Navigate to Go Goa's interactive online Booking Form or rate estimator.
  • Pick your desired car model, rental start/end dates, and preferred delivery hotspots.
  • Submit your telephone details. Our client representative will reach out on WhatsApp/Call to confirm document verification.

Benefit: Direct booking is 100% commission-free with absolutely zero hidden state charges or middleman fees.

Q2: Where is the physical Go Goa counter for Mopa Airport arrivals?

Our physical rental terminal desk is officially located at:

Counter 15, Manohar International Airport (MOPA)
Kasarvarne, Pernem District, Goa 403512, India.

We deliver vehicle handovers directly within the arrival flight parking zone block 24/7. When your flight touches down, walk straight to Counter 15; your assigned driver will hand over physical ignition keys immediately.

Q3: Which documents are mandatory for self-drive cars in Goa?

To complete safety verification and state compliance checks, the following two documents are strictly mandatory:

  • An Original Indian Driving License: Laminated, authorized category, non-learner type. We also accept DigiLocker validations.
  • A Valid Government-Issued Aadhaar Card: Serves as biometric and identity validation for our records.

Our agent will run a rapid 5-minute digital check on these documents at the precise moment of car handover. Local Goa regulations restrict bookings if original documents are unavailable.

Q4: Are there daily limits or extra charges for long drives?

Absolutely none! We provide 100% Unlimited Kilometers on all car bookings. You have complete geographical freedom to drive from historical Arambol beaches in the north to breathtaking serene coastlines in Southern Canacona. Fares remain fixed purely based on car daily rates, ensuring zero mileage stress or surprise distance calculations.

Q5: What are the speed camera rules and compliance speed limits?

The state of Goa has deployed dense, automated speed radar cameras on highways (such as the Zuari Bridge, Mandovi Bypass, and new Mopa Link road). The strict legal limit is 70 km/h to 80 km/h on state highways and 40 km/h in village and coastal zones. Violating these speed radars incurs automatic digitized traffic receipts issued directly to the vehicle registration, which are adjusted from the security reserve.

Q6: How does the security deposit refund system operate?

Go Goa acts with 100% financial compliance. We hold a small security deposit (ranging from ₹3,000 for hatchbacks to ₹5,000 for Mahindra Thar 4x4s). This deposit is fully refunded in cash or instant Google Pay / UPI transfer at the exact minute of checkout, following a standard circular vehicle paint check for scratches or body damage.
