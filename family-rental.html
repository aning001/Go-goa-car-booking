Frequently Asked and Crucial Rules for 7-Seater Rentals

Is a heavy vehicle driver's license required for 7-Seaters?

No. Under Indian Motor Vehicle Act guidelines, vehicles like Toyota Innova Crysta, Scorpio N, and Ertiga are classified as Light Motor Vehicles (LMV-NT). Any tourist holding a standard, valid non-learner passenger car driving license is legally eligible to drive these vehicles without issues.

What is the policy regarding luggage carrier racks?

If all three row seats are occupied, boot luggage spacing drops. On request, we can provide vehicles equipped with robust rooftop luggage carriers to secure bags. Please notify our coordination team at least 1 day prior to handover.
